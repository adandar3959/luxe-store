Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days within Pakistan. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available for an additional fee. International orders typically arrive within 7–14 business days.
What is your return policy?
We offer a hassle-free 14-day return policy on all items in original condition with tags attached. Simply contact us to initiate a return and we'll guide you through the process.
Do you offer size exchanges?
Yes! Size exchanges are available within 14 days of purchase. The item must be unworn and in original packaging. Shipping for exchanges is free for the first exchange on each order.
How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link via email and SMS. You can also check your order status by logging into your LUXE account and visiting the "My Orders" section.
Can I cancel or modify my order?
Orders can be cancelled or modified within 2 hours of placement. After that, the order moves to fulfillment and cannot be changed. Please contact us immediately at support@luxe.pk.
